Managing complex software systems requires careful planning of the underlying infrastructure. Many companies now rely on containerized environments to consistently deploy their applications across different environments. Service orchestration plays a central role here, as individual components are often interdependent and must be started in a specific order. Network topologies within such systems are becoming increasingly complex, especially when multiple services communicate with each other across internal networks. Data persistence is also gaining importance, as containers are inherently stateless and require external storage solutions to permanently back up data. Monitoring and logging are also essential components of robust operation, as errors must be detected and resolved early before they have a major impact on ongoing operations.
